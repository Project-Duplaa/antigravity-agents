# User Preferences & Design Mandates (Persistent)

> **Every agent MUST read this file before producing ANY visual output, code, or design specification.**
> These rules are absolute and override any conflicting skill or agent instruction.

---

## Typography Bans (Absolute)
- ❌ **NEVER** use `JetBrains Mono` — for anything, ever.
- ❌ **NEVER** use `Plus Jakarta Sans` — for anything, ever.
- ✅ Preferred serif: `Playfair Display`, `Cinzel`, or `Fraunces`.
- ✅ Preferred sans: `DM Sans` or `Outfit`.
- ✅ Monospace (only for raw data/codes): `IBM Plex Mono` or `Source Code Pro`.

## Photography & Images
- ❌ **NEVER** put images inside cards, panels, or bordered containers.
- ✅ Images are always **full-bleed editorial**: covering entire sections, bleeding to screen edges, masked with CSS gradients that dissolve into the page background.
- ✅ Photography subjects: abstract architecture, raw minerals, macro textures, aerial landscapes. Never stock photos of people smiling at laptops.

## Layout & Composition
- ❌ **NEVER** render 3 identical cards in a row. This is the #1 AI-slop tell.
- ✅ Use **asymmetric bento grids** where card sizes vary (one spans 2 cols, others span 1).
- ❌ **NEVER** use a floating translucent pill/rounded navbar.
- ✅ Use a **solid architectural masthead** with hard borders and structural weight.
- ❌ **NEVER** use `rounded-2xl` or `rounded-3xl` on cards or containers.
- ✅ Use sharp edges (`rounded-none`) or subtle radii (`rounded-md` max, 4-6px).

## Color Philosophy (Domain-Adaptive, Never Monolithic)
- ❌ **NEVER** use generic purple/pink AI gradients without domain reasoning.
- ❌ **NEVER** force every project into the same dark obsidian / amber theme.
- ✅ **The color palette MUST be bespoke and tailored to the product domain and identity**:
  - *Wellness, Spa, Nature, Organic*: Sage greens (`#8A9A86`), oat cream (`#F7F5F0`), eucalyptus, warm terracotta (`#C87D55`). Light or soft warm bases.
  - *FinTech, Wealth Management, Private Equity*: Deep navy slate (`#0B132B`), pristine alpine white (`#F8FAFC`), emerald growth (`#10B981`), platinum graphite.
  - *Luxury Fashion, High Editorial, Jewelry*: Warm alabaster/ivory (`#FAF7F2`), deep espresso/ebony (`#1A1412`), rich burgundy/wine (`#581825`).
  - *Biotech, Clinical Precision, HealthTech*: Ultra-clean sterile white (`#FFFFFF`), arctic cyan (`#0EA5E9`), icy slate, frosted glass.
  - *Gastronomy, Hospitality, Boutique*: Warm parchment (`#F4EDE2`), olive, rich black truffle (`#161311`), warm copper (`#C87533`).
  - *Creative Tools, Indie SaaS, Productivity*: Soft curated pastels, warm light canvas (`#FDFBF7`), electric cobalt (`#2563EB`) or coral accents.
  - *Extreme Hardware, Overclocking, Mission Control*: Deep obsidian slate (`#070B10`), titanium, thermal amber (`#F59E0B`), cobalt tactical.
- ✅ Match the background mode (Light, Warm Editorial, or Dark Luxury) directly to what the brand and product demand.

## Component Relevance & Strict Ban on Gratuitous Telemetry (Absolute)
- ❌ **NEVER put telemetry, sensor gauges, live coordinate crosshairs, clocks, pulse rates, or tolerance readouts on pages where they do not belong!**
  - Why would a fashion brand, a bakery, a clinic, or a standard CRM have "Z-DEPTH [mm]", "● 4Hz Live", or "DELTA T +4.8°C"? It makes zero sense and ruins product credibility.
- ✅ **Telemetry is STRICTLY CONTEXTUAL**:
  - ONLY allowed when the project is EXPLICITLY an engineering console, hardware overclocking lab, flight/satellite telemetry system, or DevOps monitoring platform.
- ✅ **Every component MUST be 100% relevant to the product vertical**:
  - *Restaurant / Gastronomy*: Seasonal tasting menu, chef's counter reservation, wine pairings, allergen badges, ingredient provenance.
  - *SaaS / B2B Software*: Interactive product workflows, feature comparisons, pricing tier matrices, ROI calculators, customer case studies, integrations.
  - *Fashion / Luxury Retail*: Lookbook gallery, fabric & weave craftsmanship, size guides, capsule collections, checkout bag.
  - *Healthcare / Clinics*: Medical specialist credentials, treatment protocols, patient intake, appointment scheduling, accredited certifications.
- ❌ Any agent putting engineering telemetry on a non-engineering page will have its output REJECTED in the Design Review Gate.

## 3D Experiences & Cinematic Product Showcases (viral-3d-experience)
- ✅ When the user asks for "3D", "secuencia", "animación tipo video", or when presenting flagship physical hardware, automotive, or luxury devices, agents MUST use the `viral-3d-experience` skill:
  - Dual-Engine architecture: Apple-style frame sequence scrubbing on `<canvas>` 2D + interactive Three.js WebGL 360° PBR scene with OrbitControls.
  - Interactive Lumafield CT Scanner slicing lenses, fluid particle physics (`THREE.Points`), and procedural Web Audio API haptics (clicks, servos, pings).
- ❌ Do NOT use heavy 3D WebGL for simple blogs, text documentation, or lightweight administrative tables where it adds useless overhead.

## Background & Motion
- ✅ The user loves **organic molten lava lamp Canvas backgrounds** — high-viscosity, non-neon, rendering in dark/amber mineral tones at 60fps.
- ✅ Motion should be deliberate, calm, purposeful. No bouncy physics.
- ✅ GSAP with `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` for micro-interactions.

## Icons
- ❌ **NEVER** use Lucide, Heroicons, or emoji as icons.
- ✅ Use **Phosphor Icons** (`@phosphor-icons/web` or `@phosphor-icons/react`).
- ✅ Weights: `duotone` for idle/sidebar, `fill` for active states.

## Functional Integrity
- ❌ **NEVER** ship dead buttons. Every button must do something (alert, toggle, expand, navigate).
- ❌ **NEVER** use placeholder data like "Acme Corp" or "Lorem ipsum."
- ✅ All data must come from the upstream PRD mock data set.

## Workflow Preferences
- ❌ **NEVER** start designing full pages or mockups blindly without first presenting an interactive component & style sampler (`design-sampler.html`).
- ✅ **Loop 0 (MANDATORY): Component & Style Sampler FIRST**: Before building the actual project mockup, the agent MUST generate an interactive `design-sampler.html` tailored to the domain.
  - The sampler lets the user visually test, click, and compare multiple options for:
    1. Typography pairings (Titular serif/sans, body, numbers mono)
    2. Button styles and tactile micro-interactions (mecanizado, biselado, wireframe, rounded)
    3. Cards, borders, and data containers (borde grueso, vidrio ahumado, monolito, abierto)
    4. Menus and navigation schemes (sidebar vertical con números, masthead superior, dock)
    5. Data displays, metrics (80px hero, gauges) and timeline/table layouts
  - The user selects their preferred visual combination and copies/pastes it in the chat.
  - ONLY AFTER the user selects their visual DNA does Loop 1 begin (building the project's actual mockup).
- ✅ **Mockup-first (Loop 1)**: The user wants to see and approve the static HTML mockup BEFORE any backend work begins.
- ✅ Mockups are standalone files that open with double-click (zero server dependencies).
- ✅ The backend adapts to the approved frontend, never the reverse.

## Copy & Language
- ❌ **NEVER** use generic SaaS buzzwords ("revolutionize", "leverage", "cutting-edge", "empower").
- ✅ Use **domain-specific technical vocabulary** appropriate to the product vertical (e.g., SWIFT MT103, UETR, Net IRR, Capital Commitment, LP for FinTech).
- ✅ Copy should be concise, confident, and precise — like a Bloomberg terminal, not a marketing brochure.

## Approved Compositional Patterns (Extracted from User Mockups)
> These patterns were designed or approved by the user. Agents should reach for these
> FIRST before inventing new patterns.

### Oversized Numbered Lists
- When listing 3-5 priority items, use **oversized mono numbers** (`text-4xl font-mono`) with a thick left border (`border-l-[3px]`) in the item's accent color.
- This creates visual weight and scanability. Small bullets are insufficient for executive interfaces.
- Each number's hover color should match its accent: `group-hover:text-amber-400`.

### Hero Metric Display
- Key percentages/metrics: display at **60-80px serif** with the unit symbol (`%`, `m`, `h`) in a smaller, muted color inline (e.g., `text-4xl text-graphite`).
- Always pair with a **label below** in mono uppercase tracking-widest.
- On the right side, show a **comparison pair**: TARGET value + OVERLOAD/DELTA value.
- Below: a **segmented progress bar** (`rounded-full`, colored segments with `gap-1`).
- Below that: a **breakdown list** (dot + label + value + percentage).
- At bottom: a **footer stats grid** (3 cols, icon + value + label).

### Section Eyebrow with Colored Bar
- Before section titles, place a colored vertical bar (`w-1.5 h-4 bg-amber-500`) + mono uppercase text (`text-[10px] tracking-[0.25em]`).
- Section title in serif (`text-5xl font-serif text-white tracking-tight`).
- Subtitle in mono uppercase graphite below.
- Counterpoint: **italic serif motto** right-aligned (e.g., `"Less noise. More progress."`).

### Timeline Stagger
- When placing blocks on a horizontal timeline, **stagger vertically** (even at `top:0`, odd at `top:32px`) to prevent visual collision and create depth.
- Each block type gets a distinct icon in a bordered container.
- Include a "NOW" vertical amber line with a hollow circle ornament + label above.

### Asymmetric Panel Composition
- Primary content (left, 8 cols): **open, no card wrapper** — breathes into background.
- Secondary panel (right, 4 cols): **IS a card** with `rounded-xl`, `backdrop-blur-sm`, darker `bg-[#0a0f16]/90`.
- This asymmetry creates hierarchy. Never wrap everything in identical cards.

### Tag Pill System
- Tag pills use **background opacity + border + text color** matching the domain:
  - `bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/20 text-amber-400` for Engineering.
  - `bg-blue-500/10 border-blue-500/20 text-blue-400` for Strategy.
  - `bg-emerald-500/10 border-emerald-500/20 text-emerald-400` for Legal.
- Priority badges use neutral styling: `border-white/10 text-graphite`.
- Font: `text-[9px] font-mono uppercase tracking-wider rounded-sm`.

### Active Nav Item
- Active sidebar item: **filled icon in a colored square** (`bg-amber-500 w-4 h-4 rounded-sm`) with icon in canvas color (`text-[#070B10]`).
- Inactive: duotone icon in graphite, transitions to white on hover.
- Number prefix: `font-mono text-[11px]` before the label.
